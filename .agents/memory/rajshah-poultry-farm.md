---
name: Rajshah Poultry Farm
description: Product context and daily operating workflow for the farm's order and billing app.
---

The product is for Rajshah Poultry Farm, a broiler chicken supplier serving shop customers. Orders are counted in whole birds and total kilograms, then priced from the daily mandi rate. The user's father sets the daily market rate.

**Why:** The app was previously a general order-billing tool; future changes must preserve the poultry supplier workflow rather than drifting back to generic retail billing.

**How to apply:** Keep broiler pieces, kilograms, daily mandi rates, shop/owner customer records, and the supplied farm identity central to future changes.
